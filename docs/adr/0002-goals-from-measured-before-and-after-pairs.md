# Auto's goals are measured from before-and-after pairs; no photos are kept

**Auto**'s goal for each scene comes from before-and-after pairs posted in photo-editing
communities, sorted by scene and screened for weak or overdone edits by an AI model, and then
measured (brightness, contrast, sky and shadow colour, saturation, and how far the edit moved the
photo). Only those measurements enter the repository. A pair shows how far a good editor moves a
photo of that scene, which a goal needs; finished photos alone are a mix of barely edited and
overdone, and earlier rounds tuned against the FiveK retouchers came out timid. The photos stay out
because the repository is public and they belong to their authors; each source and each download is
approved by the owner.

## Considered Options

- Popular finished photos only: no way to tell how much they were edited.
- The FiveK retouchers: corrections, not the bolder looks the owner wants.
- Targets written by hand, or described by an AI model: no measured agreement between editors.
