# Landscapes may be made darker under Auto

Since the v0.4 tone work, AutoEdit never makes a dark photo darker and never pushes a photo toward
clipping; those checks block or pull back an edit. Under **Auto**, a landscape whose goal calls for
it (a burned-down sunset sky, deep evening shadows) may be made darker and closer to clipping, because
the owner's examples of a good edit do exactly that, and a round that kept the old checks produced
edits the owner could not tell apart from **Simple** (owner's blind vote, 2026-11-25: no preference on
24 of 24). Checks for real faults stay on for every scene: halos, banding, blown light sources and a
wrong skin colour. **Simple** and portraits keep the old checks.

## Considered Options

- Keep the darkness and clipping checks for every style: safe, but the bold looks lose to Simple on
  sight, as the twilight round showed.
- Let the user switch the checks off: puts a technical choice on the user and still leaves Auto timid.
