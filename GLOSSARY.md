# AutoEdit

A batch photo editor that recognises what a photo shows and edits it toward a goal for that kind
of photo. These are the words the product and its plans use.

## Photos

**Scene**:
The kind of photo, as the app recognises it: people, landscape or other, with finer scenes under
each (landscape: sunset, twilight, daylight, overcast or mist). Shown to the user, who can correct it.
_Avoid_: image type, category, genre, kind

## Editing

**Goal**:
How a finished photo of one scene should look, held as measurements (brightness, contrast, sky and
shadow colour, saturation), not as slider values. Each photo is moved toward it by as much as it needs.
_Avoid_: target values, preset values

**Style**:
A named set of goals, one per scene. Built in, or saved by the user from their own fine-tuned edits.
_Avoid_: preset, look, profile

**Recipe**:
A fixed copy of one photo's settings, applied unchanged to other photos.
_Avoid_: preset, saved style

**Auto**:
The built-in style whose goals come from how good editors edit each scene, set bolder for
landscapes and subtle for portraits. Meant to become what the app gives when nothing is chosen.
_Avoid_: AI editor, automatic editor, smart edit

**Simple**:
The built-in light correction (exposure, shadows, highlights, a touch of contrast) that keeps a photo
looking as it was. The bar Auto must beat.
_Avoid_: Standard, Natural, Basic, default edit
